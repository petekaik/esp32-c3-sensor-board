Variant: compact, no relay, BME280 (routed)

Status: routed — hardware/esp32-c3-sensor-board-compact.*

Board: 34 × 76 mm, R3, 2-layer (12 mm narrower, 13 mm shorter than rev2)

What changes

  • J3, the relay module area and J4 (button terminal) removed — no relay socket, no relay footprint, no button terminal.
  • Board is a 34 × 76 mm stacked column: radar, BH1750 and BME280 one below the other, with J5 on the right.
  • GPIO3 and GPIO4 freed and added to the breakout row → J5 becomes 1×16:

3V3 · 5V · GND · GPIO0 · GPIO1 · GPIO2 · GPIO3 · GPIO4 · GPIO5 · GPIO6 · GPIO7 · GPIO10 · SDA · SCL · RX · TX

  • Jumpers JP1/JP2/JP3 unchanged with defaults 1–2 (I²C, 0x76, 0x23).

BOM

../BOM.csv minus the J3 socket, J4 terminal and KY-019 relay module; J5 is the 1×16 socket (LCSC C7499334).
