Pin map and connectors

Authoritative electrical reference for the carrier board. Rev2 is the ordered baseline; rev3 adds the 1×16 J5. I²C is on GPIO8 (SDA) / GPIO9 (SCL), UART on GPIO20/21, relay GPIO4, button GPIO3. doorbell.yml still lists GPIO6/7 for I²C and must be updated to sda: GPIO8 / scl: GPIO9.

Connectors

U2 — LD2410C (1×5 socket)

1 TX · 2 RX · 3 OUT · 4 GND · 5 VCC (5 V)

Module silkscreen order is TX RX OUT GND VCC; nets are crossed on purpose (module TX → UART_RX).

J1 — BME280 / BME680 (1×6 socket)

1 3V3 · 2 GND · 3 SCL · 4 SDA · 5 CSB · 6 SDO

CSB is pulled to 3V3 through JP1 (I²C mode); SDO selects the address through JP2.

Module compatibility. A 4-pin BME280 uses pins 1–4 (VCC, GND, SCL, SDA) and leaves CSB/SDO unused; verify it accepts 3.3 V and check its fixed address. The common CJMCU-680 BME680 is ordered VCC, GND, SCL, SDA, SDO, CS, so its last two pins are swapped versus J1; use a 6-pin crossover adapter or a CS-before-SDO clone.

J2 — BH1750 GY-302 (1×5 socket)

1 3V3 · 2 GND · 3 SCL · 4 SDA · 5 ADDR

ADDR to GND through JP3 → 0x23.

J3 — relay (1×3 right-angle socket, KY-019 class)

1 GND · 2 5V · 3 RELAY (GPIO4) — module pin order GND, Vcc+, Signal.

Not fitted on the compact variants.

J4 — button (2-pin screw terminal)

1 BUTTON (GPIO3) · 2 GND — internal pull-up, button shorts to GND. Relay variants only; compact variants omit J4 and use GPIO3 on J5.

J5 — breakout row (1×16 female socket, rev3)

Fitted with a standard 2.54 mm 1×16 female socket (Connector_PinSocket_2.54mm:PinSocket_1x16_P2.54mm_Vertical, LCSC C7499334).

3V3 · 5V · GND · GPIO0 · GPIO1 · GPIO2 · GPIO3 · GPIO4 · GPIO5 · GPIO6 · GPIO7 · GPIO10 · SDA · SCL · RX · TX

GPIO3/GPIO4 are also on J4/J3 on the relay variants. Rev2 uses the older 1×14 row; compact variants drop J3/J4 and use the full 1×16 row on the 34 × 76 mm compact board.

Jumper defaults

RefDefaultMeaning
JP11–2CSB tied to 3V3 → I²C mode (2–3 = CSB to GPIO7, SPI mode)
JP21–2SDO tied to GND → address 0x76 (2–3 = SDO to GPIO5, SPI MISO)
JP31–2ADDR tied to GND → 0x23 (open = 0x5C)

All three are 2.54 mm pin headers; fit a shunt on the marked pair. The default 1–2 pair is ticked on the bottom silkscreen, where the pins are numbered (1–3 for JP1/JP2, 1–2 for JP3) and the JP1/JP2/JP3 columns are named. Pin 1 is the square pad.
