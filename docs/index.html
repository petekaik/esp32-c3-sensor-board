ESP32-C3 Sensor Board

Wall-mountable carrier for an ESP32-C3 Super Mini — plug-in sensor modules, relay and button terminals, USB-C on the top edge.

Final boards

Variants

Relay + BME280 rev2 built / rev3 routed

46 × 89 mm. Relay, BME280, BH1750, LD2410C, button. Rev2 has the 1×14 J5; rev3 expands it to 1×16 (GPIO3/GPIO4).

Relay + BME680 PCB ready

46 × 89 mm, rev3 1×16 J5. BME680 on the 1×6 socket; CJMCU-680 needs a 5/6 crossover.

Compact + BME280 routed

34 × 76 mm stacked column: radar, BH1750 and BME280, full 1×16 breakout row on the right.

Compact + BME680 PCB ready

34 × 76 mm stacked column; BME680 module and firmware variant.

Case and faceplate

The compact board has a 41 × 84 × 22.5 mm wall-mount case printed from case/compact_tray.stl and case/compact_cover.stl (press-fit lip plus four M3 screws, two keyholes, a discreet radar/BME hole mesh, a Ø5 BH1750 sight hole and side vents). The PCB sits 0.5 mm from the side walls and flush with the USB end. The optional stone faceplate sits on the cover.
