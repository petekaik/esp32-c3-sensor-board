# Assembly BOM / CPL

The carrier board is **through-hole only**: the provider fits the sockets, headers and terminals, except **J3** (the raised relay socket), which is hand-soldered. The ESP32-C3 Super Mini, LD2410C, BME280, BH1750 and relay are **plug-in modules** and are *not* part of the assembly — insert them after the board arrives.

## What is assembled

| Designator | Part | Qty | LCSC |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1A, U1B | 2.54 mm 1×8 female header | 2 | C27438 |
| U2, J2 | 2.54 mm 1×5 female header | 2 | C50950 |
| J1 | 2.54 mm 1×6 female header | 1 | C40877 |
| J4 | 5.0 mm 2P screw terminal (MX126-5.0-02P) | 1 | C5188434 |
| J5 | 2.54 mm 1×14 female header | 1 | C41417326 |
| JP1, JP2 | 2.54 mm 1×3 male pin header | 2 | C49257 |
| JP3 | 2.54 mm 1×2 male pin header | 1 | C32713268 |
| J3 | 2.54 mm 1×3 right-angle female header, long pins (hand-solder) | 1 | C54876732 |
| SHUNT | 2.54 mm jumper shunt (hand-fit) | 3 | C5305 |
| H1–H4 | 3.2 mm NPTH mounting holes | — | no part |

**J3 is hand-soldered, not assembled.** The relay socket must be raised to meet the KY-019 module's header; automated THT assembly places parts flush. J3 is therefore excluded from every provider BOM/CPL — see [hand-solder-j3.md](hand-solder-j3.md). The three jumpers' shunts are also hand-fitted after assembly.

The Aisler project `BEAGDVXB` (`esp32-c3-sensor-board-rev2`) has catalogue parts
assigned to all ten assembled lines; J3 is marked excluded. Aisler substitutes
a Samtec `SSW-114-01-T-S` for J5 because LCSC `C41417326` is not in its
catalogue. See [`docs/pricing.md`](../../docs/pricing.md) for the live BOM.

**U1 is two 1×8 sockets**, not a 2×8 header: the Super Mini's two pin rows are 15.24 mm apart, so they are fitted as `U1A` and `U1B`. **U2 is a 1×5 socket** for the LD2410C; the module itself is not assembled.

## Provider files

- `vendor-neutral-bom.csv` — one row per designator with **LCSC** and **Aisler
  MPN** columns filled; the five plug-in modules are listed as excluded.
- `jlcpcb/bom-jlcpcb.csv`, `cpl-jlcpcb.csv`, `gerbers-jlcpcb.zip`,
  `netlist-jlcpcb.ipc` — generated by **Fabrication Toolkit 5.3.1** from the
  `LCSC` field on each footprint (`plugins.cli -nI -t -f -e`). The toolkit's
  `bom.csv`/`positions.csv` become `bom-jlcpcb.csv`/`cpl-jlcpcb.csv`; **J3 is
  filtered out of both** because it is hand-soldered. Upload
  `gerbers-jlcpcb.zip` together with the BOM and CPL — the toolkit's CPL and
  gerber zip share the same origin. The same BOM/CPL are mirrored at
  `fabrication/bom-jlc.csv` and `fabrication/cpl-jlc.csv`.
- `pcbway/bom-pcbway.csv`, `cpl-pcbway.csv` — PCBWay format:
  `Designator,Qty,Value,Footprint,MPN,LCSC` and
  `Designator,Mid X,Mid Y,Layer,Rotation`. Positions are part-centre,
  bottom-left origin, Y up (board height 89 mm). J3 omitted.
- `aisler/bom-aisler.csv`, `cpl-aisler.csv` — generic / Aisler format:
  `Designator,Qty,Value,Footprint,MPN` and the same CPL columns. Aisler may
  ask for their own template; the part data is the same.

## Notes

- The JLCPCB positions come from the Fabrication Toolkit and are **part-centre**,
  not pad-1 origins. The toolkit's CPL and its gerber zip share one origin;
  upload them as a set. The PCBWay CPL is converted to bottom-left/Y-up for
  that portal. Check each provider's placement preview.
- U2 carries an `FT Rotation Offset` of 90° because it uses the custom
  `ld2410c` footprint; the toolkits cannot infer the equivalent standard-socket
  rotation from that name.
- The LCSC parts are JLCPCB **extended** parts, so an assembly setup fee may
  apply. Stock was checked on 2026-10-31; re-check at order time.
- The modules are not in JLCPCB's assembly library. Source them separately and
  plug them into the sockets after assembly.
- Regeneration command (KiCad 10, macOS) is in [`HANDOFF.md`](../../HANDOFF.md);
  the toolkit is installed under
  `~/Documents/KiCad/10.0/3rdparty/plugins/com.github.bennymeg.JLC-Plugin-for-KiCad`.
